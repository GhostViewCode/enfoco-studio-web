02 How it works

Four steps. You never have to be at the property.

  1. Step 01

    Send the photos you already have

    Phone photos are fine. If none of them are usable, we send you our photo guide: a four-page PDF that walks you through shooting them yourself in twenty minutes, with no gear and no photography skills.

  2. Step 02

    Get your test video in 48 hours

    A short tour of your property, built from your photos. Not a template, not somebody else's example. Yours, ready to publish.

  3. Step 03

    Like it, you pay. Don't, you owe nothing

    No small print and no sales call in between. Tell us by email or WhatsApp and that's the end of it.

  4. Step 04

    Everything arrives ready to upload

    Photos and videos in the exact format each platform expects, via a private download link. No watermarks, and no cropping left for you to do.

No visits. No schedules to juggle.

There are two people behind this, not a contact form.

We're a small studio based in Mallorca, working across Europe from a desk.

We only work with holiday rentals. No weddings, no products, no portraits.
