1. What a cookie is

A cookie is a small file a website stores on your device when you visit. It's used to remember things about your visit: the language you chose, whether you've dismissed a notice, or how you move around the site.

2. Cookies this site uses

In its current configuration this site sets no first-party or third-party cookies, and uses no analytics or advertising tools. Language preference is handled through separate URLs (/es/ and /en/), so nothing needs to be stored in your browser.

4. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies from your browser settings. Every browser handles it slightly differently — search for "cookies" in the settings of Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge. Blocking cookies will not stop you using this site.

5. Third-party services
